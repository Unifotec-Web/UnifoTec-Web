# Content governance

Only approved, attributable company content may be published.

## Approval required

- Team members, roles, biographies, portraits, and social profiles
- Client names, projects, case studies, outcomes, logos, and testimonials
- Performance, uptime, security, compliance, pricing, and service claims
- Contact details, office locations, legal pages, and social URLs

## Claim-verification policy

Publish capability descriptions only when they describe work that can be scoped and agreed. Do not publish client or project counts, outcomes, fixed timelines, uptime, response-time, security, scalability, performance, or compliance guarantees without documented approval and evidence. Estimates, support arrangements, controls, and service levels must be described as scope-dependent and confirmed in the quotation or written agreement.

## Team presentation

Until individual identity approval is recorded, the public team presentation is role-only. It may list the approved roles but must not include names, portraits, biographies, social profiles, or implied employment claims.

## Current status

The company story, mission, vision, values, delivery process, differentiators, and service descriptions are AI-assisted launch drafts based on the approved company direction. They require final business-owner review before production deployment.

Sample posts, fictional testimonials, invented metrics, and unapproved individual team profiles are not published. The Projects page may show clearly labelled concept showcases that demonstrate capabilities, but they must never be presented as client work or evidence of outcomes. Verified case studies, testimonials, metrics, and individual profiles remain pending approval.

Policy copy is an operational starting point and must be reviewed against the company's actual quotation, payment, cancellation, and refund practices before online payments are enabled.

## Review record

For each approved item, retain its source, approving owner, approval date, expiration/review date, and any client consent restrictions. Do not place private approval records or client data in this repository.
